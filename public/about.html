Learning that keeps going when the data runs out

SabiPack is an AI-powered, offline-first learning companion for Nigerian students. Wale Lab NextGen AI Hackathon 2026, Track 1.

Try the AI

Paste a few sentences of study notes. The AI turns them into a short lesson and quiz. After that, the lesson and quiz keep working with no internet.
